## How to Add Posts

Simply create a new `.md` file in the `src/content/blog/` folder on GitHub with this structure:

```markdown
---
title: Your Post Title
description: Brief description
pubDate: 2024-07-24
author: Your Name
tags: [tag1, tag2]
---

# Your Content Here

Your markdown content...
```

That's it! Your post will appear automatically on the blog page and home page.
